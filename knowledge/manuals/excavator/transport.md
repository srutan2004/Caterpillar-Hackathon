# Transport and Lifting (Transport)

This section describes the safe handling of a generic excavator during road transport. It covers three core activities:

1. **Loading the excavator onto a trailer**  
2. **Securing the machine with tie‑down straps**  
3. **Using the designated lifting points for hoisting**

All procedures are written for a standard‑size excavator with an operating mass of **21 500 kg** (conventional diesel) or **22 800 kg** (electric‑assist). Use the value that matches the machine you are moving.

---

## 1. Loading on a Trailer

### PROCEDURE 001: Position the Trailer

1. Park the trailer on a level, firm surface free of debris.  
2. Verify that the trailer’s rated payload exceeds the excavator’s operating mass (≥ 22 800 kg for the electric version).  
3. Engage the trailer brakes and chock the wheels.

### PROCEDURE 002: Align the Excavator

1. Drive the excavator forward until the front of the under‑carriage is directly over the trailer’s longitudinal centreline.  
2. Use the machine’s rear‑wheel steering (if equipped) or a spotter to keep the tracks centered on the trailer deck.  

> **WARNING:** Do not allow any part of the under‑carriage to overhang the trailer edges. Over‑hang can cause loss of balance during lift‑off.

### PROCEDURE 003: Lower the Machine onto the Trailer

1. Activate the hydraulic lowering function (or use the manual winch if the model lacks a hydraulic lowering valve).  
2. Lower the excavator slowly until all wheels or tracks rest on the trailer deck.  
3. Confirm that the under‑carriage is fully supported by the trailer’s load‑bearing surface; no point should be bearing directly on the trailer frame.

### PROCEDURE 004: Check Clearance

| Item | Minimum Required |
|------|-------------------|
| Front over‑hang from trailer nose | within acceptable limits |
| Rear over‑hang from trailer tail | within acceptable limits |
| Side clearance to trailer side rails | sufficient to avoid contact |

If any dimension exceeds the limits, reposition the excavator before proceeding.

## 2. Tie‑Down (Securing the Machine)

### PROCEDURE 010: Select Appropriate Tie‑Downs

| Tie‑down type | Minimum breaking strength* |
|---------------|----------------------------|
| Heavy‑duty polyester webbing (5‑inch width) | 30 kN |
| Steel chain with grade‑8 shackles | 40 kN |

\*Select a tie‑down whose breaking strength is **≥ 1.5 ×** the excavator’s operating mass divided by the acceleration due to gravity (i.e., a very high‑rated component). In practice, use the highest‑rated webbing or chain available.

### PROCEDURE 011: Attach Primary Restraints

1. Locate the **front‑frame tie‑down points** (usually reinforced eyelets on the under‑carriage).  
2. Thread a webbing strap through each eyelet, then run the strap over the trailer’s front cross‑member.  
3. Tighten the strap using a ratchet until a pre‑load of the appropriate proportion of the strap’s rated strength is achieved.

### PROCEDURE 012: Attach Secondary Restraints

1. Identify the **rear‑frame tie‑down points** on the under‑carriage.  
2. Connect a second set of straps or chains to the trailer’s rear cross‑member, mirroring the front arrangement.  
3. Apply the same pre‑load as in the previous step.

### PROCEDURE 013: Add Lateral Restraints

1. Use two additional straps to prevent side‑to‑side movement.  
2. Run each strap from a lateral under‑carriage eyelet, across the trailer deck, and secure to the opposite side rail.  
3. Tension each strap to the same pre‑load level.

> **WARNING:** Never use damaged or frayed straps. Inspect all webbing, hooks, and shackles for cracks, corrosion, or excessive wear before each use.

### PROCEDURE 014: Verify Tie‑Down Integrity

1. Perform a “push‑test”: gently push the excavator forward, backward, and side‑to‑side while observing strap tension.  
2. All straps must remain taut with no visible slack.  
3. Re‑tighten any strap that shows a loss of pre‑load beyond the acceptable tolerance.

## 3. Lifting Points (Hoisting the Machine)

### PROCEDURE 020: Identify Approved Lifting Points

| Location | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Front lifting eye | Reinforced steel eye on the under‑carriage front frame |
| Rear lifting eye | Reinforced steel eye on the under‑carriage rear frame |
| Central lifting pad | Flat, load‑rated pad on the machine’s centre of gravity (CG) |

> **WARNING:** Only use the manufacturer‑approved lifting points. Improper points can cause structural failure.

### PROCEDURE 021: Determine Lifting Capacity

1. Calculate the **required lifting force**:  

   \[
   F = \frac{m \times g}{\text{Number of points used}}
   \]

   Where **m** = operating mass (21 500 kg or 22 800 kg) and **g** = standard gravity.  

2. Select a hoist or crane with a **minimum rated load** of **≥ 1.25 × the total machine weight** (i.e., a capacity exceeding the operating mass by a safety factor).  

### PROCEDURE 022: Attach Lifting Equipment

1. Connect a **grade‑8 steel shackle** to each approved lifting eye.  
2. Thread a **rated lifting chain or synthetic rope** through the shackles.  
3. Verify that each chain/rope’s working load limit (WLL) exceeds the calculated per‑point force.

> **WARNING:** Do not exceed the WLL of any lifting component. Over‑loading can cause sudden failure.

### PROCEDURE 023: Perform a Lift Test

1. Slowly raise the excavator a few centimeters off the trailer deck.  
2. Observe the machine for any unexpected movement, twisting, or deformation of the lifting points.  
3. Hold the lift for a short period to confirm stability, then lower back to the deck.

### PROCEDURE 024: Execute the Transport Lift

1. With the hoist/crane positioned directly over the centre of the trailer, raise the excavator to a height that clears the deck.  
2. Maintain a **steady, controlled lift**; avoid jerky motions.  
3. Once the machine is clear, proceed to the next transport step (e.g., loading onto a transport vehicle or moving to a storage area).

### Summary

- **Operating mass**: 21 500 kg (diesel) or 22 800 kg (electric).  
- **Trailer payload** must exceed the machine’s mass.  
- **Tie‑down**: Use heavy‑duty webbing or grade‑8 chains, pre‑load appropriately, and verify with a push‑test.  
- **Lifting points**: Use only approved eyes/pads; calculate per‑point force and select a crane with at least 1.25 × total weight capacity.  
- **Safety**: Perform visual inspections, lift tests, and obtain documented sign‑offs before moving the machine.
