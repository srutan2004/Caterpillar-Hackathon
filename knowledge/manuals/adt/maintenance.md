## Maintenance Intervals – Articulated Dump Truck

This section describes the scheduled maintenance program for the articulated dump truck. Follow the procedures exactly and record all work in the equipment log. All time estimates are **estimated labor hours (est_h)** as listed in the specification table.

| Service Item | Hours Between Service | Typical Labor (h) | Main Tasks |
|--------------|----------------------|-------------------|------------|
| **service_250h** | 250 h | 2.0 | Engine oil, oil filter, greasing |
| **service_500h** | 500 h | 4.5 | Transmission filter, outer air filter, brake check |
| **service_1000h** | 1 000 h | 7.0 | Transmission oil, axle oil, suspension check |
| **service_2000h** | 2 000 h | 9.0 | Hydraulic oil, coolant, body pins |

> **NOTE:** The “Hours Between Service” values are cumulative. For example, the 500‑hour service includes all tasks from the 250‑hour service plus the additional items listed for 500 h.

### PROCEDURE service_250h: 250‑Hour Basic Service  

1. **Preparation**  
   - Park the truck on level ground, engage the parking brake, and chock the wheels.  
   - Allow the engine to cool before opening any fluid caps.  

2. **Engine Oil Replacement**  
   - Remove the oil drain plug and drain the oil into a suitable container.  
   - Replace the drain plug with a new washer.  
   - Fill the crankcase with the manufacturer‑specified engine oil to the correct level.  

3. **Oil Filter Change**  
   - Remove the old oil filter with an appropriate filter wrench.  
   - Lubricate the gasket of the new filter with a small amount of fresh engine oil.  
   - Install the new filter hand‑tight, then apply the torque specified in the service manual.  

4. **Greasing**  
   - Apply the recommended grease to all chassis, suspension, and articulation points listed in the **Greasing Chart** (see below).  

5. **Final Checks**  
   - Verify oil level with dipstick.  
   - Start the engine, let it idle for a short period, then re‑check for leaks.  

> **WARNING:** Never open the oil filler cap while the engine is hot. Hot oil can cause severe burns.

**Estimated labor:** 2.0 h  

---

### PROCEDURE service_500h: 500‑Hour Intermediate Service  

1. **Complete all tasks from the 250‑hour service** (engine oil, oil filter, greasing).  

2. **Transmission Filter Replacement**  
   - Locate the transmission filter housing.  
   - Remove the housing bolts, extract the old filter, and discard it in accordance with environmental regulations.  
   - Install a new filter, ensuring the sealing gasket is correctly seated.  
   - Re‑install the housing and torque bolts to specification.  

3. **Outer Air Filter Service**  
   - Open the air‑filter housing.  
   - Remove the outer filter element, inspect for damage, and replace if necessary.  
   - Clean the housing interior with a lint‑free cloth.  

4. **Brake System Check**  
   - Visually inspect brake drums, pads, and lines for wear or leakage.  
   - Measure brake drum wear; replace if the minimum thickness is exceeded.  

5. **Documentation**  
   - Record filter part numbers, oil quantities, and any observations in the maintenance log.  

> **WARNING:** The transmission contains high‑pressure fluid. Wear protective gloves and eye protection when removing the filter housing.

**Estimated labor:** 4.5 h  

---

### PROCEDURE service_1000h: 1 000‑Hour Major Service  

1. **Perform all tasks from the 500‑hour service** (engine oil, oil filter, greasing, transmission filter, outer air filter, brake check).  

2. **Transmission Oil Change**  
   - Warm the transmission to operating temperature to improve oil flow.  
   - Drain the transmission oil into a clean container.  
   - Replace the drain plug and refill with the specified transmission oil to the correct level.  

3. **Axle Oil Replacement**  
   - Locate the axle oil fill and drain plugs.  
   - Drain each axle, replace any worn seals, and refill with the recommended axle oil.  

4. **Suspension Check**  
   - Inspect all suspension links, bushings, and shock absorbers for cracks, corrosion, or excessive play.  
   - Replace any components that do not meet the tolerance criteria in the service manual.  

5. **Functional Test**  
   - Operate the articulation and dump mechanisms at low speed to confirm smooth operation.  

> **WARNING:** Axle oil is under high pressure; use a catch pan and wear safety glasses.

**Estimated labor:** 7.0 h  

---

### PROCEDURE service_2000h: 2 000‑Hour Overhaul Service  

1. **Execute all tasks from the 1 000‑hour service** (engine oil, oil filter, greasing, transmission filter, outer air filter, brake check, transmission oil, axle oil, suspension check).  

2. **Hydraulic Oil Replacement**  
   - Depressurize the hydraulic system following the lock‑out procedure.  
   - Drain the hydraulic reservoir and replace the filter if equipped.  
   - Refill with the specified hydraulic oil, checking for correct viscosity and cleanliness.  

3. **Coolant System Service**  
   - Drain the cooling system completely.  
   - Flush the radiator and engine passages with clean water until runoff is clear.  
   - Refill with the manufacturer‑specified coolant mixture.  

4. **Body Pin Inspection & Replacement**  
   - Remove each body pin, clean the pin bore, and inspect for wear, cracking, or corrosion.  
   - Replace any pin that shows signs of damage.  

5. **Comprehensive Inspection**  
   - Verify the condition of all components listed under **components** in the specification (engine, transmission, brakes, tyres, axles, articulation, dump_body, dpf, cooling, electrical).  
   - Perform any corrective actions required before returning the truck to service.  

> **WARNING:** Hydraulic oil is flammable. Keep ignition sources away and work in a well‑ventilated area.

**Estimated labor:** 9.0 h

## Tyre & Track Inspection (Applicable to All Services)

1. **Visual Inspection**  
   - Look for cuts, sidewall bulges, tread wear, and embedded objects.  

2. **Tread Depth Measurement**  
   - Use a tread‑depth gauge; minimum acceptable depth is as specified by the tyre manufacturer.  

3. **Inflation Check**  
   - Verify tyre pressure against the pressure plate on the data plate; adjust as required.  

4. **Wheel‑Bolt Torque**  
   - After any tyre removal or wheel‑nut adjustment, torque wheel bolts to the specification.  

5. **Track (if equipped) – Chain Tension**  
   - Check chain tension with the chain‑tension gauge; adjust to the recommended slack.  

> **WARNING:** Incorrect tyre pressure or under‑tensioned tracks can lead to premature wear, loss of traction, and possible vehicle instability.
