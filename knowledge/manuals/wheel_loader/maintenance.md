# Wheel Loader – Maintenance Intervals  
*Operator & Maintenance Manual – Section 4*

## 1. Introduction  

Regular maintenance keeps the wheel loader operating safely, efficiently, and within emissions limits. The intervals below are based on **operating hours (h)** recorded on the machine’s hour‑meter. All tasks must be performed by qualified personnel using the tools and consumables specified in the Parts & Service catalogue.  

> **WARNING:** Performing service outside the prescribed intervals can lead to premature component wear, loss of performance, and may void the warranty.

## 3. Detailed Maintenance Procedures  

### PROCEDURE 250A: 250‑Hour Service – Engine Oil, Oil Filter & Greasing  

1. **Preparation**  
   - Park the loader on a level surface, engage the parking brake, and shut off the engine.  
   - Allow the engine to cool for a short period.  
   - Place a suitable drain pan beneath the oil sump.  

2. **Engine Oil Drain & Replacement**  
   - Remove the oil drain plug and let the oil flow completely into the pan.  
   - Replace the drain plug with a new torque‑spec washer and tighten to **30 Nm**.  
   - Fill the engine with the manufacturer‑approved oil to the **“Full”** mark on the dipstick.  

3. **Oil Filter Replacement**  
   - Unscrew the oil filter housing, discard the used filter, and clean the mounting surface.  
   - Install a new filter, applying a thin coat of fresh oil to the gasket. Tighten to **25 Nm**.  

4. **Greasing of Designated Points**  
   - Locate all greasing points listed in the Service Manual (typically 12–16 points covering pivot joints, axle bearings, and linkage bearings).  
   - Apply **high‑temperature grease** using a grease gun until fresh grease appears at the discharge port.  

> **WARNING:** Over‑greasing can cause excess heat buildup and seal damage. Apply only the amount that forces grease out of the fitting.

5. **Final Checks**  
   - Start the engine, let it idle briefly, then check for oil leaks.  
   - Verify oil pressure on the gauge panel.  

**Estimated labor time:** 2.0 h  

---

### PROCEDURE 500B: 500‑Hour Service – Hydraulic, Transmission & Air Filters  

1. **Hydraulic Filter Replacement**  
   - Depressurize the hydraulic system by cycling the controls to the neutral position and opening the relief valve.  
   - Remove the hydraulic filter housing, discard the old filter, and clean the housing interior.  
   - Install a new filter, ensuring the O‑ring is seated, and tighten to **15 Nm**.  

2. **Transmission Filter Replacement**  
   - Drain the transmission oil into a clean container.  
   - Remove the transmission filter cover, replace the filter element, and reinstall the cover with a torque of **20 Nm**.  

3. **Outer Air Filter Replacement**  
   - Open the engine compartment and locate the outer air filter housing.  
   - Remove the housing cover, discard the used filter element, and install a new one with the correct orientation.  
   - Re‑secure the housing cover.  

4. **Re‑fill Fluids**  
   - Re‑fill the hydraulic system with the specified hydraulic oil to the **“Full”** level.  
   - Re‑fill the transmission with the recommended transmission oil to the **“Full”** mark.  

5. **System Checks**  
   - Cycle the loader controls through all ranges, listening for abnormal noises.  
   - Verify that the hydraulic pressure gauge reads within the normal operating range.  

**Estimated labor time:** 4.0 h  

---

### PROCEDURE 1000C: 1 000‑Hour Service – Transmission Oil, Axle Oil & Brake Check  

1. **Transmission Oil Change**  
   - Warm the transmission to operating temperature to improve oil flow.  
   - Drain the transmission oil via the drain plug into a suitable container.  
   - Replace the drain plug with a new washer and torque to **35 Nm**.  
   - Re‑fill with the specified transmission oil to the “Full” level.  

2. **Axle Oil Change**  
   - Locate the axle oil drain plugs on each axle.  
   - Drain the oil, replace the drain plug washers, and tighten to **30 Nm**.  
   - Fill each axle with the recommended axle oil to the “Full” mark.  

3. **Brake System Inspection**  
   - Visually inspect brake drums, pads, and lines for wear, cracks, or leakage.  
   - Measure brake pad thickness; replace if below the minimum specification.  
   - Check brake fluid level in the reservoir; top up with the specified brake fluid if low.  

4. **Functional Test**  
   - Perform a low‑speed brake test in a safe area, confirming firm pedal feel and even braking on both sides.  

**Estimated labor time:** 6.0 h  

---

### PROCEDURE 2000D: 2 000‑Hour Service – Hydraulic Oil & Coolant  

1. **Hydraulic Oil Replacement**  
   - Follow the same depressurization steps as in Procedure 500B.  
   - Drain the hydraulic oil completely.  
   - Replace the hydraulic oil filter (see Procedure 500B).  
   - Re‑fill the system with fresh hydraulic oil to the **“Full”** level.  

2. **Coolant System Service**  
   - Allow the engine to cool, then open the coolant drain plug and let the coolant flow into a collection container.  
   - Flush the cooling system with clean water until runoff is clear.  
   - Close the drain plug, then fill with the specified coolant to the **“Full”** mark on the radiator expansion tank.  

3. **Air Bleed**  
   - Bleed air from the hydraulic system using the bleed valves on each circuit until a steady stream of oil emerges.  
   - Bleed the cooling system by opening the bleed screw on the thermostat housing and allowing trapped air to escape.  

4. **Final Verification**  
   - Start the engine, let it reach normal operating temperature, and check for leaks.  
   - Verify hydraulic pressure and coolant temperature gauges are within limits.  

**Estimated labor time:** 8.0 h

## 6. Tyre / Track Inspection  

> **NOTE:** This loader is equipped with pneumatic tyres; if a tracked version is in use, substitute “track” for “tyre” in the steps below.  

1. **Visual Inspection (Every )**  
   - Check tyre sidewalls for cuts, cracks, or bulges.  
   - Verify tread depth is greater than the minimum specified.  

2. **Pressure Check (Every)**  
   - Measure tyre pressure with a calibrated gauge.  
   - Adjust to the pressure listed on the data plate.  

3. **Wheel Nut Torque (Every 250 h)**  
   - Loosen each wheel nut, inspect the hub and bolt threads.  
   - Re‑tighten to **120 Nm** using a torque wrench.  

4. **Alignment Check (Every 1 000 h)**  
   - Perform a wheel alignment test using a laser or mechanical alignment tool.  
   - Adjust toe and camber to the specifications in the Alignment Chapter.  

> **WARNING:** Operating the loader with under‑inflated or damaged tyres can cause loss of stability, increased fuel consumption, and tyre failure.
