## Safety  

The following safety guidance applies to **all** wheel‑loader operations.  It is written for a generic machine and must be read together with the operator’s manual, site‑specific risk assessments and local regulations.  When a numeric value is required, the exact figure from the specification table is used – no approximations or invented numbers are permitted.

### PROCEDURE S01: Identify General Hazards  

1. Conduct a **pre‑start site walk‑around** to locate:  
   * Uneven ground, soft spots, or slopes that could affect stability.  
   * Overhead power lines, underground utilities, and any visible obstacles.  
2. Verify that the **working envelope** (the area the loader can reach while the bucket is lowered) is clear of personnel and equipment.  
3. Check weather conditions. If **lightning** is observed or forecasted, initiate the **stop‑work** protocol (see Procedure S06).  

> **WARNING:** Operating a wheel loader on a surface with a side‑slope greater than **12 °** (caution) or **17 °** (warning) dramatically increases the risk of rollover.  

---

### PROCEDURE S02: Establish Exclusion Zones & Pedestrian Control  

1. Mark the **advisory exclusion zone** at **20 m** from the loader’s centre of rotation with high‑visibility tape or cones.  
2. Mark the **caution zone** at **10 m**. Personnel may enter this zone only with the operator’s explicit permission and when the loader is stationary.  
3. Mark the **warning zone** at **5 m**. No person may enter this zone while the loader is moving or the bucket is raised.  

| Zone | Radius | Allowed Activity |
|------|--------|------------------|
| Advisory | 20 m | Observation only |
| Caution | 10 m | Entry only with permission, loader stopped |
| Warning | 5 m | **Strictly prohibited** while operating |

2. Use a **spotter** when work must be performed inside the caution zone. The spotter must wear high‑visibility clothing and maintain constant visual contact with the operator.  
3. Communicate any changes to the zone layout to all site personnel before beginning work.  

> **WARNING:** Breaching the **5 m warning zone** while the bucket is raised can cause sudden load shifts and loss of control.

### PROCEDURE S03: Seat‑Belt Use & ROPS (Rollover Protective Structure)  

1. **Before engine start**, fasten the seat‑belt.  
2. The system provides a **grace period of 5 s** after the belt is released before a reminder chime sounds.  
3. If the belt remains unfastened for **20 s** (escalation period), the machine will automatically **reduce travel speed to 5 km h⁻¹** and display a visual warning.  
4. Never operate the loader with the seat‑belt unfastened, even when the ROPS is engaged. The ROPS is designed to protect the operator **only** in a rollover event; it does **not** substitute for seat‑belt use.  

> **WARNING:** Failure to wear the seat‑belt is a primary cause of fatal injuries in rollover accidents.  

---

### PROCEDURE S04: Working Near Utilities & Overhead Lines  

1. Identify all **underground utilities** (water, gas, electrical) using site plans and utility locators.  
2. Maintain a minimum clearance of **5 m** from known underground services unless a protective barrier is installed.  
3. For **overhead power lines**, keep the loader’s highest point (usually the bucket tip) at least **5 m** below the line.  
4. If the line voltage is **above 1 kV**, treat it as a **high‑voltage hazard** and follow the **high‑voltage safety** steps (Procedure S07).  

> **WARNING:** Contact with an overhead line can result in electric arc flash, severe burns, or fatality.  

---

### PROCEDURE S05: Weather & Lightning Stop‑Work  

1. Monitor real‑time weather alerts. When **thunderstorms** are forecast or lightning is observed within **5 km**, initiate the stop‑work sequence:  
   * Bring the loader to a complete stop in a **level, open area** away from structures.  
   * Lower the bucket to the ground and engage the **parking brake**.  
   * Shut off the engine and remove the key.  
2. Wait at least **30 minutes** after the last observed lightning strike before restarting operations.  

> **WARNING:** Operating a wheel loader during a lightning storm can cause electrical damage to the machine and pose a lethal risk to the operator.

### PROCEDURE S06: High‑Voltage Safety for Electric Variants  

*(Applies only to electric wheel‑loader models; skip if operating a diesel‑powered unit.)*  

1. Verify that the **battery pack** is isolated by the **high‑voltage interlock** before performing any maintenance.  
2. Use insulated tools rated for **≥ 1000 V** when working near high‑voltage components.  
3. If a **high‑voltage fault** is suspected (e.g., unusual arcing, smoke, or a fault indicator), follow the emergency shutdown:  
   * Press the **emergency stop** button.  
   * Wait **5 s** (grace period) for the system to discharge.  
   * Only qualified personnel may approach the battery after the **discharge timer** has elapsed.  

> **WARNING:** Contact with the high‑voltage system can cause severe electric shock or fatality.

### PROCEDURE S07: Stability & Load Management  

1. **Stability Margin** – Keep the calculated stability margin **above 1.8** (advisory). If it drops to **1.5** (caution) reduce speed, lower the bucket, and reassess the load. If it reaches **1.25** (warning), stop immediately, lower the load, and re‑position the machine.  
2. **Side‑Slope Operation** – Do not operate on slopes steeper than **12 °** (caution). If the slope approaches **17 °** (warning), cease operation and relocate to a flatter area.  
3. **Travel Speed with Bucket Raised** – Do not exceed **8.0 km h⁻¹** when the bucket is lifted. Reducing speed improves stability and reduces hydraulic stress.  
4. **Payload Ratio** – The ratio of actual load weight to the rated bucket capacity must stay **≤ 1.0** (caution). If the ratio exceeds **1.1** (warning), stop, lower the load, and redistribute material.  

| Parameter | Advisory Limit | Caution Limit | Warning Limit |
|-----------|----------------|---------------|---------------|
| Stability margin | ≥ 1.8 | ≥ 1.5 | < 1.25 |
| Side‑slope | ≤ 12 ° | ≤ 17 ° | > 17 ° |
| Travel speed (bucket raised) | – | ≤ 8.0 km h⁻¹ | – |
| Payload ratio | – | ≤ 1.0 | > 1.1 |

5. Monitor the **hydraulic relief pressure**; the system will vent at **285 bar** to protect components. If the pressure gauge approaches this limit, reduce load or stop the machine and inspect the hydraulic circuit.  

> **WARNING:** Exceeding any of the caution or warning limits can cause loss of control, equipment damage, or operator injury.

### PROCEDURE S08: Emergency Evacuation & First‑Aid  

1. If a **rollover**, **collision**, or **electrical incident** occurs, activate the **emergency alarm** and call site emergency services.  
2. Keep the **first‑aid kit** and **fire extinguisher** (rated for Class B & C fires) within arm’s reach of the operator’s seat.  
3. Perform a **head‑to‑toe** visual inspection of all personnel before resuming work.  

> **WARNING:** Delayed evacuation after a rollover can trap the operator and increase the risk of crush injuries.  

---

### PROCEDURE S09: Documentation & Reporting  

1. Record every **stop‑work**, **zone breach**, and **warning‑limit exceedance** in the equipment logbook.  
2. Submit a **hazard report** to the site safety officer within 24 hours of any incident or near‑miss.  
3. Review the log monthly to identify trends and implement corrective actions.
